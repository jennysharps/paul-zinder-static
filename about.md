Paul is a filmmaker who makes documentaries about intriguing people and fascinating cultural events.  His award-winning work has screened at over 80 international venues, including the National Gallery of Art (Washington D.C.).

He’s also a writer who specialises in film genre and cult television.  Paul edited the collection <a title="The Multiple Worlds of Fringe: Essays on the J.J. Abrams Science Fiction Series" href="http://www.amazon.com/Multiple-Worlds-Fringe-Science-Fiction/dp/0786475676/ref=sr_1_1?s=books&amp;ie=UTF8&amp;qid=1433589206&amp;sr=1-1&amp;keywords=multiple+worlds+of+fringe" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><cite>The Multiple Worlds of </cite>Fringe<cite>: Essays on the J. J. Abrams&#8217; Science Fiction Series</cite></a> (with Tanya R. Cochran and Sherry Ginn), which was published by McFarland in 2014.

Paul's writing also appears in <cite>Women in the Western</cite> (Edinburgh University Press, 2020), <cite><cite>Justified</cite> and Philosophy</cite> (Open Court Press, 2015), <cite>Contemporary Westerns: Film and Television since 1990</cite> (Scarecrow Press, 2013), <cite>The Last Western: <cite>Deadwood</cite> and the End of American Empire</cite> (Bloomsbury Academic, 2013), <cite>Investigating <cite>Veronica Mars</cite>: Essays on the Teen Detective</cite> Series (McFarland, 2011), and <cite>Investigating <cite>Alias</cite>: Secrets and Spies</cite> (I.B. Tauris, 2007).
